Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone and Apple Watch, on iOS 26. The first 250 recordings will be free.

Easy and quick recording

Record anytime, anywhere, from your iPhone or Apple Watch. A widget, the Control Centre button or the Action Button all start a note in one tap. No Wi-Fi needed, even for the transcript.

Summarized and detected reminders

Turn any shower thought, rambling or half-formed idea into an ideal note. Everything is summarized, and to-dos are picked out for you to keep.

Synced with Apple Reminders

Kept to-dos are sorted by theme into the Reminders lists you already use, so your to-do list feels contextual rather than bolted on.

Organized for easy search

Search a specific word, or roughly what you meant. Browse by month to zoom out, and a year in review to wrap it up. Topics show what keeps coming back.

Made simple to get out of your way

A quieter interface keeps the attention on what matters: saying the thing, and reading it back later. There are no folders to file notes into and no tags to remember. You talk, and the rest sorts itself out.
